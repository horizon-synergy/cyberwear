Transparency

Plain language about how things are made.

We are not claiming perfection. We are building a clearer path toward improved materials, responsible partners, and production choices that can be explained.

Materials

Starting with responsible blends

Early pieces may use recycled polyester or blended fibers to reduce waste and keep production accessible while we continue testing organic and regenerative alternatives.

Manufacturing

Ethical partners first

Supplier selection prioritizes safe working conditions, small-batch feasibility, durable construction, and willingness to share sourcing details.

Care

Lower-impact ownership

Care notes will favor cold washing, air drying, repair, and long-term wear so the environmental conversation continues after purchase.

Progress

Updates without exaggeration

Material improvements, production constraints, and seasonal experiments will be documented as they happen, not polished into claims we cannot support.
